📜 Auction rules — the honest version

  • Sellers ship the item to the winner after payment clears — the Plaza never holds or ships items.
  • All auction sales are final. No returns, no refunds — bid only on items you truly want.
  • No counterfeits, ever. List only genuine items you own and can ship. Fakes get your selling privileges pulled.
  • 18+ only — listing and bidding are adults-only, like the rest of the party.
  • Bids go up in $1 minimum steps. A bid in the last 5 seconds adds 5 seconds, so nobody gets sniped at the buzzer.
  • The Plaza keeps a 10% cut of each auction sale (separate from the 30% gift cut) — sellers see both in their earnings.
