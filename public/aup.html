Rizz Plaza — Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") explains what is and isn't allowed on Rizz Plaza, the 18+ social and live-streaming platform at rizzplaza.com. It sits alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy — by using the Plaza you agree to all three. If you don't agree, please don't use the Plaza.

Adults only

Rizz Plaza is for adults only. You must be 18 years old or older to create an account or use the Plaza in any way. Don't create an account for someone under 18, don't share your account with a minor, and don't try to get around the age gate or any age-verification request. Misrepresenting your age is grounds for immediate termination, as set out in the Terms of Service.

Keeping people safe

  • Sexual content involving a minor is zero tolerance. Accounts are closed and the matter is reported to the appropriate authorities.
  • Don't harass, bully, threaten, or stalk another member — in rooms, in live broadcasts, in comments, or in direct messages.
  • Don't post content that is unlawful, or that promotes illegal activity, violence, or self-harm.

Your content and copyright

  • Only post photos, videos, music, and other content that you created or have the right to post. Don't post someone else's copyrighted work as your own.
  • Every video and audio upload is scanned automatically for copyrighted material. Where a match is found, the infringing audio is muted or removed; repeat infringement can lead to account action.
  • You may repost content from another platform only where that platform's rules and the original creator's settings allow it. If downloads or sharing are turned off at the source, it stays off the Plaza.

Honest accounts and commerce

  • Don't impersonate another person or brand, or create misleading accounts.
  • No counterfeits, ever. In auctions and in the Plaza Store, list only genuine items you own and can ship. Selling fakes can cost you your selling privileges and your account.
  • Don't sell or promote illegal goods, or anything unlawful.
  • Don't spam, scam, or run misleading promotions, and don't abuse the platform's systems — including the gift, auction, charm, and payout systems — or try to manipulate earnings, pots, or rankings.

Reports and enforcement

  • Every profile, post, and player card has a Report option, and reports are reviewed by the platform owner. Photos may also be checked automatically for prohibited content before or after posting.
  • We may remove content, end a live broadcast, or suspend or close accounts that break this policy or the Terms of Service.
  • Members can block each other in Messages; blocked members can't message you, and you can't message them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above. Continuing to use the Plaza after a change means you accept the updated policy.
