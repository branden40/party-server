Rizz Plaza — Child Safety Standards

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Rizz Plaza ("we," "us," "the Plaza") is an online social platform at rizzplaza.com with live streaming rooms, live auctions, community chat, dating and social rooms for adults, and a supervised all-ages teen space. These standards state our published position and practices against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Zero tolerance

Child sexual abuse and exploitation are strictly prohibited on Rizz Plaza in any form. This includes creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, linking to, or describing CSAM or the sexual exploitation of a minor, and any attempt to groom, solicit, or arrange contact with a minor for sexual purposes. An account involved in any of this is permanently banned, the material is removed, and the matter is reported as described below. There are no warnings and no appeals for CSAE violations.

Prevention built into the platform

  • Age floor and age gates. Members must be 13 or older to join. Dating rooms, Faith Dating, auctions, one-to-one messaging, and all money features are restricted to members verified as 18 or older by date of birth, enforced on the server.
  • Teen protections. Members under 18 cannot use direct messages, cannot take part in dating or auctions, and cannot buy, earn, or cash out money — their activity is limited to a virtual charms-only economy. Adults cannot privately message teen members.
  • A supervised teen space. Teen Hangout is an all-ages room with additional enforcement that applies there: an automatic profanity filter (a violation removes the sender from the room for a cooldown period) and an on-device photo safety check on shared photos.
  • Content screening. Photos and videos posted to the Plaza are screened on the device before publishing, and nudity is not permitted anywhere on the platform. Nudity or sexual content in a live ends the live immediately and can result in a permanent ban.

Reporting child safety concerns

Every member can report child safety concerns from inside the app: profiles, posts, comments, and live rooms all carry a Report option, and reported content can be hidden immediately while it is reviewed. Child safety reports are treated as the highest priority. Anyone may also report a concern directly by email to support@rizzplaza.com.

Our response

When we become aware of possible CSAM or child exploitation, we act promptly: the content is removed, the account is suspended or permanently banned, available evidence is preserved as required by law, and the material is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and, where appropriate, to law enforcement. Rizz Plaza complies with applicable child safety laws, including U.S. federal law requiring electronic service providers to report apparent CSAM to NCMEC.

Designated contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety matters — able to speak to our CSAM prevention practices and to receive notices from platform providers and authorities — is reachable at support@rizzplaza.com.
