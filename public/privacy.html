Rizz Plaza — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Rizz Plaza ("we," "us," "the Plaza") is an online social platform at rizzplaza.com with live streaming rooms, live auctions, community chat, and creator earnings. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions about this policy or your information: [email protected].

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up we collect your name or display name, a unique username, email address, phone number, and date of birth. Your date of birth is used to verify age for areas of the Plaza that are limited to members 18 and older. Passwords are stored only as a salted cryptographic hash — never in plain text.
  • Profile and content you share. Profile photos, videos, reels, and workout videos you upload; your direct messages (kept so you and the other person can read them later); your live broadcasts and the chat you send in rooms and lives, which go out live to the people there — room and live chat is not stored by us afterwards; and — if you use the Faith Dating feature inside Christian Fellowship — your faith profile and questionnaire answers.
  • Payment and earnings information. If you buy charms or a Premium subscription, or cash out earnings as a host, we keep records of those transactions (amounts, charm balances, gifts, and payout status), along with your Stripe account reference if you connect one for cash-outs. Card and bank details are collected and processed by Stripe, our payment provider — we do not receive or store your full card number or bank account number.
  • Support and sign-up forms. Messages you send through Help & Support, and the email address or phone number you give us (with your consent) for event reminders and invites.
  • Technical information. Like most online services, our servers automatically record basic technical data such as your IP address, browser or device type, and connection logs, which we use to run and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To create and run your account and let you use rooms, live streaming, auctions, messaging, and dating features.
  • To verify age where the law or our rules limit a feature to adults (18+).
  • To process purchases, gifts, subscriptions, and host cash-outs.
  • To keep the Plaza safe — including automated review of uploaded photos for prohibited content, and responding to reports of abuse.
  • To answer support requests and, where you consented, send reminders and invites.

When information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers needed to run the Plaza, for example:

  • Stripe — to process payments, subscriptions, and host payouts, and to verify hosts who cash out.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — to store and serve the site, your photos, and your videos.
  • Content moderation services — when that check is switched on, uploaded photos may be sent to our moderation provider (currently Hive AI) to be checked automatically for prohibited content.

Other members can see the public parts of your profile — your display name, username, profile photo, and the content you post in public rooms; your name and gift totals can also appear on the daily rankings board. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our members.

Cookies and device storage

Rizz Plaza stores small pieces of data on your device (such as your signed-in session and your preferences, including your age-verification status) so you stay signed in and the app remembers your settings. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Age rules

Rizz Plaza is for adults only: every member must be 18 years old or older, and the date of birth on an account must be the member's own. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if an account is found to belong to someone under 18, it is disabled.

Your choices

  • You can update your profile information in the app at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated personal information by emailing [email protected]. Some transaction records may be kept where the law or our payment provider requires it (for example, payout and tax records).
  • If you joined the reminders and invites list, you can take yourself off it at any time with the unsubscribe option or by contacting support.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable technical measures — including encrypted connections and hashed passwords — to protect your information, and we keep personal information only as long as needed to run the service, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
